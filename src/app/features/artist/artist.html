Rishi began her practice in her grandmother's vanity, learning the ancient grammar of kohl, jasmine and silk. Today she leads Glow by Rishi — a small atelier built on a single belief: that beauty should feel like you, only quieter and more luminous. Trained in Mumbai and London, certified in airbrush and HD techniques, and a devoted student of traditional Indian draping and mehendi, Rishi tailors every look to your features, your fabric and your story.
